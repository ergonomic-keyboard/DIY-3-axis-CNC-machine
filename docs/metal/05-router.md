## Attach router to carriage

Instead of the plastic P24 router bracket, the Makita router (E20) is clamped to the sliding plate **M36.b** using the two metal clamps **M24.a** (bottom) and **M24.b** (top).

The two clamps embrace the router barrel. Four M4×40 mm screws (S11) draw the clamps together through M36.b's front face (see 3D assembly for bolt positions).
