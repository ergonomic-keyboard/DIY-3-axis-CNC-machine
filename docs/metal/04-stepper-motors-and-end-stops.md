# Metal — Stepper motors and end-stops

## Metal components

| M-code | Human name | Replaces | Script object | Status |
|--------|-----------|----------|---------------|--------|
| M40.a | engine holder top plate | P40 | `Top_Stepper_Holder` | **metal** (see page 03) |
| — | all other parts | — | — | unchanged |

All stepper motor wiring, end-stop installation, and cable management is identical to the [plastic build](../04-stepper-motors-and-end-stops.md).

The only difference: the Z-axis stepper motor (E11) mounts to **M40.a** (engine holder top plate, metal) instead of P40 (3D-printed Z motor mount). The bolt pattern and motor dimensions are the same (4× M3×8 mm, S08).

Follow the [plastic stepper motors and end-stops instructions](../04-stepper-motors-and-end-stops.md) in full.
