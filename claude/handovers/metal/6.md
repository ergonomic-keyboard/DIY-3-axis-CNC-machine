## 2. What got implemented in `assemble_and_render.py`

### Component I — aluminium frame (DONE + rendered)
- **`add_beam()`** new primitive: hollow 2 mm-wall extrusion (outer box minus inner box, open
  along the long axis) with a `holes=[{axis,u,v,d[,depth]}]` list cut via `_hole_through`/`_hole_blind`.
  Replaced the solid `add_box` frame members + posts. Shell fraction ≈ 24.9% (verified).
- Vertical **M8 tie rods** (`_add_frame_tie_rods`) through the 8 posts, **recessed** so nothing
  protrudes past the bars; washers side-load (explode along X). Wrench-access counterbores (Ø16) on top.
- 4 long **horizontal tie rods** (`_add_frame_side_rods`) run in **Y** through the **Front/Back**
  beams (deliberately rotated from the Left/Right beams so they share no beam with the verticals →
  no crossing). Their wrench holes are on the **SIDE** (axis Y) of the long Left/Right beams, one per corner.
- `POST_X = (60,285,585,810)` — outer posts inset from the corners via `CORNER_INSET=60`.
- Frame-rail screws, gantry-rail screws, p2of2-rail bolts added earlier (`_add_frame_rail_bolts`,
  `_add_gantry_rail_bolts`, `_add_p2of2_rail_bolts`). Gantry beams also hollowed (component III).

### Component II — side plate & clamps (WIP — the active area)
Was 4 parts, now **3**: `Side_Plate_Left` (imported STEP "mid plate") + `Side_Plate_Front_Clamp`
(the two upper-beam clips **fused** into one U-bridge, trimmed to 10 mm Y) + `Side_Plate_Beam_Clamp`.
- The beam clamp is a **modeled** thin-Y **U-fork** (`Part.makeBox` + notch cut, U opening +X).
  The imported clip's U-outtake did **not** survive the 10 mm Y-trim (its arms were on the Y faces),
  so it was remodeled: 25(X)×10(Y)×72(Z), notch 18 deep × 32 tall (fits 30 mm beam).
- **Tie thread** (block `(d)` in the SIDE PLATES section): M8 stud in **X** at `TIE_Y=-3`
  (the mid plate's Y) and world Z≈147 (≈ middle of the two upper beams). Runs through the clamp's
  **U-floor hole** and an **X-bore in the mid plate**; washer+nut each end. The beam clamp was
  seated to Y=-3 so the U-floor hole, plate bore, and thread are coaxial.
- New helpers for editing imported STEP shapes: `_read_shape`, `_scale_about_center`,
  `_rotate_about_center`, `_thin_axis`, `add_shape_obj`, plus local `_xbore`.

### Every sub-component GIF now shows an X/Y/Z reference triad
`_place_reference_triad()` resizes+moves the `Axis_*` triad to a corner of each sub-component's
bbox (world-oriented, red=X/green=Y/blue=Z), so it turns with the orbiting camera. Wired into
`_render_subcomponent_inner`. All 7 sub-component gifs re-rendered with it.

## 3. Conventions / gotchas learned this session (important)
- **Coords:** X=depth(0→900), Y=width(0→793, centre 396.5), Z=vertical. Clamp/plate shapes are
  built in LOCAL coords; `add_shape_obj`/`add_step` add **z=93** ⇒ world Z147 = local Z54.
  Right-side parts are `_R`, mirrored about Y=396.5.
- **Rotation direction is a footgun.** "CW looking into −Z" = **−90°** right-handed about Z;
  "+90 right-handed / thumb +Z" = **+90°**. Confirm with a top-down (view direction `(0,0,-1)`)
  render — a Z-rotation preserves the Z bbox dimension.
- **Thin BEFORE rotating.** `_rotate_about_center` bakes rotation via `transformGeometry`; a
  near-90° matrix leaves a µ-tilt so an axis-aligned slab cut afterwards fails to shrink the bbox.
  So `_thin_axis` on the axis that becomes Y *before* the rotation.
- **Classification:** object names drive `_classify_subcomponent`. Names containing `Side_Plate`
  → II (or II_R if they end `_R`); `_Tie_` in a frame-rod name → I. Keep this in mind when naming.
